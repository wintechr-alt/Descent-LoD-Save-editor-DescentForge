Misc

Puts back any missing "no part" options, marks the recipes crafted for items you own, removes duplicate items, recipes and list entries, combines repeated material and shop stacks, keeps only the upgraded version when both are owned, and drops recipes the original editor wrote for bought items. You'll see the full list before anything changes.

    Reset to default

    Puts items, recipes, materials, gold, the shop, skills, feats and every hero's gear back to how a new Act 1 campaign starts. Story progress, party name, XP and anything else are kept.

    ITEM OR RECIPE FOR SALE

    Items & recipes for sale

    Each is sold once. Items you own and recipes you know can't be added, so buying can't make a duplicate.

    Only what you can still add is listed: items you own and recipes you know are left out. Weapons are grouped by hero, with each weapon's part A, part B and part C like the hero pages.

    "Add everything" puts every item and recipe listed below on sale (Act 2 ones only when the campaign allows them). That can be a few hundred entries, which hasn't been tried in the game, so try it on a copy of your save first. "Clear all" takes them all off again.

    Enemy weaknesses

    Tick an enemy to reveal all its weaknesses and resistances, or untick it to forget it. Solid tags are revealed. Dashed tags are still hidden.

    • The small "flags" number under an enemy is what the save stores for it. One part of that number (bit 0) isn't understood and is ignored; it's set whenever an enemy is revealed.
    • The weaknesses and resistances come from DescentForge's enemy list. Enemies in a save that aren't on that list are never changed.
